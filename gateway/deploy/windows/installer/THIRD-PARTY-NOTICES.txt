Node Goblin for Windows packages an unmodified Node.js executable from the
official Node.js win-x64 distribution. Its version, distribution URL, and
archive SHA-256 are recorded in package-build.json. The Node.js license and
bundled dependency notices are in node\LICENSE.

The Node Goblin project license is included in LICENSE.txt.
